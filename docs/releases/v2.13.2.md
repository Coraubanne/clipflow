## ClipFlow 2.13.2

### [Download ClipFlow for macOS](https://github.com/Coraubanne/clipflow/releases/download/v2.13.2/ClipFlow-Installer.dmg)

Direct MP4 files detected on pages such as Hostinger now receive a professional filename based on the originating website and the page title. Opaque Cloudflare/CDN identifiers are no longer exposed as the creator and title.

The Hostinger case was validated end to end: the embedded 720p H.264 source downloads without a slow video conversion and imports into Premiere Pro.
